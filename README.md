# Project Fenrir

A semi-open-world action RPG for iOS.

> *Fate determines your beginning. Choice determines your evolution. Mastery determines your destiny.*

## Overview
Players are randomly assigned an elemental affinity at awakening. Through behavior — combat, exploration, decisions, creature interactions — their element evolves into unique forms that reflect how they played, not what they selected.
